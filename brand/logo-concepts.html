Brand exploration · Round 1

WisePestControl — logo concepts

Three directions for a Florida pest control company, each with a palette sampled from the logo that carries straight into the website. Two of them build on the owl, the natural symbol of wisdom and nature's own pest control. The third is a monogram for a more classic, green look. Every mark is vector, holds up at favicon size, and works on light and dark backgrounds.

01

Night Watch

Clever · watchful · modern

An owl that is also a house. The butterfly roof of a Florida mid-century home becomes the owl's brow, the round porthole windows become its eyes, and the stilts become its feet. The message: a wise expert keeping an eye on your home. Midnight blue and warm amber give it a calm, premium feel. On the site, the hero turns into a night scene where a flashlight beam finds the pests hiding in the dark.

02

Homegrown

Friendly · green · family-first

The W of Wise doubles as a house. Its middle peak is the roof over a sunny yellow door, and its last stroke grows into a leaf. It feels friendly and family-first without the usual bug-in-a-red-circle clichés, and the monogram looks sharp on trucks, uniforms and yard signs. Fresh greens and sunshine yellow keep it bright and approachable. On the site, a line of ants marches toward the house and turns back at the protective barrier.

03

Florida Native

Local · charming · retro

The Florida burrowing owl, the little ground owl that lives in open sandy fields across the state and eats a lot of bugs, standing guard at its burrow under the Florida sun. It works as a mascot as much as a logo: trucks, uniforms, stickers and social posts. A retro badge and a bold script make it the most local, most memorable option of the three, in sky, sun and sand. On the site, the owl stands in the hero and turns its head to follow the cursor.
