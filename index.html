How it works

Outside first. Inside only if you need it.

Most pests live outdoors and walk in. So we stop them at the edge of your home, the same way on every visit.

  1. 1

    Free inspection

    A licensed tech walks your property, finds how pests are getting in, and shows you exactly what they found.

  2. 2

    Seal the gaps

    We seal the openings around pipes, A/C lines, doors and weep holes that pests use as a front door.

  3. 3

    Build the barrier

    A targeted treatment along your foundation, eaves and entry points turns pests back before they reach the walls.

  4. 4

    Keep it strong

    Quarterly visits refresh the barrier and clear webs and wasp nests from the eaves. Pests back in between? So are we, free.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are your treatments pet-friendly?

We use targeted, low-impact products and treat mostly outside your home. Your tech will tell you exactly when pets and kids can head back out, which is usually as soon as the treated areas are dry.

Do I need to be home for service?

No. Most visits are outside only. If you'd like interior service, we'll book a time that suits you. Either way, you'll get a text before we arrive and a photo report after.

How often will you come out?

General pest control is quarterly. Mosquito service is monthly from March to November, and termite stations are checked on the schedule set in your plan.

What if I see pests between visits?

Call or text us. If pests come back between scheduled visits, we'll come back and re-treat at no extra charge.

Do you do termite inspections for home sales?

Yes. Our licensed inspectors complete Florida WDO (wood-destroying organism) reports, usually within 24 to 48 hours so your closing stays on schedule.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Plans run quarter to quarter, and you can cancel any time.
